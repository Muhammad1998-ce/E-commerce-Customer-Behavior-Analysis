Customer Behavior & RFM Analysis Dashboard
📌 Project Overview

This project provides a comprehensive 360-degree analysis of customer behavior for an e-commerce business. By integrating sales data, demographic profiles, and transaction history, the dashboard uncovers critical insights into revenue trends, customer retention, and operational challenges like high return rates.

The analysis is divided into five strategic pillars:

    Executive Overview: High-level KPIs and revenue trends.

    Customer Segmentation (RFM): Identifying loyal vs. at-risk customers.

    Gender & Product Behaviors: Demographic-based purchasing patterns.

    Return Analysis: Root cause analysis of product returns.

    Churn Analysis: Predicting and understanding customer attrition.

📊 Key Business Insights

    Revenue Performance: Generated a total of $113.33M in sales, though a significant drop was observed in 2023.

    Segment Risk: Identified that the "At-Risk" and "Lost" segments surprisingly represent the highest historical revenue ($26.7M combined), signaling an urgent need for win-back campaigns.

    Operational Bottleneck: The Return Rate is 40.59%, with the highest impact coming from the Senior demographic (50+) and high-priced items.

    Churn Alarm: The "Good" customer segment shows a churn rate of 20.44%, indicating that even high-value customers are leaving the platform.

🛠️ Technical Toolkit

    Data Visualization: Power BI .

    Analytics Techniques: RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) Clustering, Trend Analysis, and Churn Prediction.

    DAX/Calculations: Custom measures for CLV (Customer Lifetime Value), AOV (Average Order Value), and Churn %.

Recommendations

    Retention Strategy: Implement a targeted loyalty program for the "Good" segment to reduce their 20.44% churn rate.

    Quality Control: Audit the "Home" category and products priced above $100 to investigate the high return volumes.

    Senior Engagement: Re-evaluate the user interface (UI) or product sizing guides to better cater to the 50+ age group, who show the highest return and churn rates.
